The Service

Kanavu Illam offers automated architectural analysis tools: reading your 2D floor plan, building a 3D parametric model, traditional Tamil Vastu rule scoring, and preliminary structural and cost (bill of quantities) estimates, with a downloadable PDF report. The Service is a planning aid, not a professional engineering or architectural service.

3

Accuracy and Professional Disclaimers

  • Automatic plan reading: Floor plans are read by computer-vision software (YOLO, OpenCV and EasyOCR). It can misread labels, hand-drawn lines, walls or dimensions. You must check every dimension, room boundary and site orientation yourself before relying on any output.
  • Vastu: Vastu scores follow traditional cultural rules and are given for information and cultural reference only. They are not architectural, engineering or legal advice.
  • Cost estimates: Material prices, labour rates and totals are indicative planning figures. Real costs vary with suppliers, location, site conditions and the market. Live price and brand search results are indicative and may come from an automated language model.
  • Structural guidance: Column, beam and footing suggestions are preliminary planning figures based on commonly used Indian Standard formulas (such as IS 456 conventions). They do not replace soil testing, municipal approvals, or drawings signed by a licensed structural engineer.
Important Building Notice: You must not build from the report alone. Always get a qualified, licensed professional structural engineer or architect to verify the design before on-site construction.
