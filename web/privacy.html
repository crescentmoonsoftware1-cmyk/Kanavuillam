Privacy Policy for Kanavu Illam

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Kanavu Illam ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details our data practices in full compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules 2011, and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Email address, name, and optional phone number registered through Supabase authentication.
  • Temporary Image Data: 2D architectural blueprint sketches or drawings uploaded solely for immediate geometric scanning.
  • Payment Identifiers: Secure order and transaction tokens processed via PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (Razorpay). We do not store sensitive bank account numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

2. Immediate Zero-Storage Architecture

We maintain an Immediate Zero-Storage operational policy. Uploaded floor plan images are processed statelessly in-memory and via temporary disk caching by local computer vision algorithms (OpenCV). Immediately upon completing the analysis and delivering the report to the user, all uploaded files are permanently unlinked and erased from our servers.

3. Final Report Ownership

All computed outputs (3D CAD visualizations, traditional Tamil Vastu scores, structural engineering load assessments, and cost estimations) are provided directly to the user as a downloadable PDF document stored exclusively on the user's private device storage.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users maintain the absolute right to request permanent deletion of their account credentials and personal profile records at any time. Deletion can be initiated directly inside the application under Profile > Delete Account, or through our public deletion portal at: Request Account Deletion.
