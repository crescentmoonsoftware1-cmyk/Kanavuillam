How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To analyse your floor plan and generate your 3D model, Vastu score, cost estimate and structural guidance.
  • To save your projects so you can open them again in History.
  • To process payments and issue records.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, enforce rate limits, and keep the Service reliable.
  • To respond to your support requests and grievances.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
5

How We Handle Your Floor Plan Drawings

Immediate Zero-Storage Architecture: Uploaded floor plan image files are stored temporarily on the server disk only while automated computer-vision software (YOLO object detection, OpenCV, and EasyOCR text reading) processes them. The files are deleted immediately after processing, or at server restart if processing was interrupted. We do not keep raw floor plan images, and we do not keep image links in our database. Your drawings are never sent to OpenRouter or any external language model.
6

Project Results Stored in Your Account

The results derived from your drawings are stored in our cloud database under your unique user ID so that you can see them in History. Access to these records is controlled by our backend, which checks your signed-in session, together with database access policies. They stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account.

8

Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your account data and projects only while your account is active.

  • Delete inside the app: Go to Profile → Delete Account. You must be signed in. Your profile, projects, 3D models, Vastu and cost results and history are deleted immediately, and your sign-in account is removed.
  • Delete from the web: If you cannot open the app, go to https://kanavuillam.in/delete-account, enter your account email, and confirm the one-time code or link we send to that email. We verify ownership this way so nobody can delete someone else’s account. After verification, deletion is completed within 7 days, and we email you when it is done.
  • What we keep after deletion: Payment and billing records (transaction IDs and the billing email attached to them) are kept only for the period Indian accounting, tax and GST laws require, then deleted. Copies in routine backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Data on your device: Anything stored only on your device (session tokens, language, optional address, downloaded PDF reports) stays there until you clear app data or uninstall. We cannot delete files you saved outside the app.

Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to erase your data (see Section 8).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to act.
  • Get your grievance resolved by our Grievance Officer (Section 13).

To use these rights, email grievance@kanavuillam.in from your registered email address or use the in-app delete option. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and respond within 15 days. If you are not satisfied after using this route, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and operational.

10

Security Safeguards

We use industry-standard safeguards: encryption of data in transit (TLS), signed-in session checks on protected endpoints, access controls and database policies on project records, rate limiting on sign-in and expensive features, and deletion of uploaded drawings immediately after processing. No system is completely secure. If we confirm a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

Children's Privacy
